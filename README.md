# ⚡ AI Inference, Credit Depletion & Contract Pricing Deal Desk Model

A Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) and Deal Desk decision simulator built in Python and Streamlit. Designed specifically to evaluate direct AI model inference costs, vector database storage scaling, multi-tier contract pricing, Pareto-skew user distributions, promotional credit burn velocity, and working capital cash lags.

---

## 🎯 Key Features & Capabilities

* **Founder Onboarding & Stage Presets:** Quickly switch between *Pre-launch*, *Early Traction*, and *Scaling* profiles to populate baseline assumptions.
* **Granular Enterprise Cohort Tracking:** Evaluates sales-led multi-month contracts with vintage cohort retention, account expansion, and strict contract expiration mechanics.
* **Advanced Inference Cost Routing:** Dynamically models traffic splits between frontier and standard models, cache hit-rate savings, batch pricing ratios, and token growth curves.
* **Accounts Payable & Cash Lag Ledger:** Reconciles P&L inference costs against promotional credit grants (with expiration tracking) and accounts payable cash disbursement lags (0 to 3 months).
* **Quantitative Pricing Archetype Engine:** Compares *Pay-As-You-Go*, *Subscription + Allowance*, *Hybrid Base + Overage*, and *Prepaid Credit Burndown* models on a like-for-like paid volume and cost basis.
* **Interactive Deal Desk Governance Tabs:**
  1. 📊 **Revenue & Margin** (Multi-tier P&L and unit economics snapshots)
  2. 💳 **Credits & Cash** (Grant drawdown, expiration velocity, and working capital cash flow)
  3. 📋 **Monthly Detail** (Full roll-forward ledger table)
  4. 📈 **Scenario Matrix** (Multi-scenario comparative analysis)
  5. 📑 **Audit Register** (Data governance and source mapping)
  6. 🎯 **Value & Pricing Strategy** (Value-creation anchor calculator and archetype comparison)
